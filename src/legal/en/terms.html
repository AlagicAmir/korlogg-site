2. What the App Does

Körlogg detects and records car trips using your phone's location and motion sensors, lets you classify trips as business or private, and produces reports (PDF and CSV) laid out after the information that the tax authority of the country rules you choose asks for in a mileage log (for example Skatteverket in Sweden, the Finanzamt in Germany, HMRC in the United Kingdom or the IRS in the United States). Distances are shown in kilometres or miles, depending on the country rules you choose (miles for the United Kingdom and the United States). It also calculates mileage allowance or reimbursement from rates you can edit.

3. Accuracy of Recorded Trips

GPS and motion detection are not perfect. Trips can be missed, merged, split or measured a little short or long, especially in tunnels, cities with tall buildings, or when the phone's battery-saving settings limit background activity. You are responsible for reviewing and correcting the log before relying on it, and for entering odometer readings. We recommend comparing the log with your odometer regularly. We are not liable for tax, reimbursement or other consequences of an inaccurate or incomplete log.

Corrections and the change log. The distance (kilometres or miles) and odometer readings of a trip can be corrected for 7 days after the trip, then they are fixed. For vehicles under Austrian or German rules the whole trip is fixed after those 7 days, trips still unsorted then count as private, and trips cannot be deleted, only cancelled with a reason. Every change made by hand is recorded and printed in the export, as tax authorities expect of an electronic log.
