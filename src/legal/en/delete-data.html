Request data deletion

App: Körlogg — mileage log for drivers in Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland, Germany, Austria, the Netherlands and Poland. Developer: Amir Alagic, enskild firma, Sweden (EU).

What data exists

Your trips, vehicles, places and settings are stored on your device, and in your own iCloud account if you turned on iCloud sync. We do not have a copy of either. The only data held outside your device is a random app user ID and purchase records at our purchase processor, RevenueCat, if you have made or restored a purchase.

Delete data on your device

  • Delete individual trips by swiping them in the list. For a car on German or Austrian rules the log is kept the way the Finanzamt requires, so a trip is cancelled with a reason instead of deleted; delete the app or the iCloud data (below) to remove everything.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app from your phone (iPhone or Android).
  • With iCloud sync on, deleting trips in the app deletes them from iCloud too. To remove the whole iCloud copy, open Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage on the iPhone and delete Körlogg's data.

Delete purchase data held by our processor

Email support@korlogg.com with the subject "Körlogg data deletion". To find the record we need the app user ID shown at the bottom of Settings in the app, or the date and the Apple receipt reference or Google Play order number (GPA.…) of your purchase. We will delete the RevenueCat record within 30 days and confirm by email. Note that deleting the record makes it impossible to restore a purchase on a new device; Apple and Google keep their own purchase history under their own policies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure and portability of personal data we process, and lodge a complaint with the Swedish Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).
