Start your trip log the moment your iPhone connects to the car

Körlogg has two actions for Apple's Shortcuts app: Start trip and End trip. Tie them to CarPlay or to your car's Bluetooth, and your log starts when you get in and ends when you get out. You set it up once, in a couple of minutes, with the car you already have.

What you get

  • A log from the first metre. The trip starts when the phone connects to the car, before you pull away.
  • Your own car, by name. With Bluetooth you pick your car from a list, so the automation belongs to that car.
  • A clean end. With a second automation the trip is saved to your log when the phone disconnects. Körlogg also ends a trip by itself once the car has stood still for a few minutes.
  • Siri too. Say “Start a trip in Körlogg” or “End the trip in Körlogg”.
  • Included for everyone. Both actions are part of Körlogg 3.1 and later, in the free version as well. Shortcuts is Apple's own app on your iPhone.

Before you start

  • Körlogg 3.1 or later.
  • Location access “Always”. That lets a trip start while Körlogg is closed. Under Settings in Körlogg you see the location status, with a button that takes you to the right switch.

Start the trip when the phone connects

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap Automation at the bottom.
  2. Tap New Automation – or the + at the top right if you already have automations.
  3. Choose CarPlay if your car has CarPlay. Otherwise choose Bluetooth, tap Choose next to Device and pick your car.
  4. Keep the option for connecting selected: Connects for CarPlay, Is Connected for Bluetooth.
  5. Choose Run Immediately, so the trip starts without a question on the screen. Then tap Next.
  6. Scroll to Körlogg in the list, tap it and tap Start trip.

The automation is saved and appears on the Automation screen. The next time your phone connects to the car, Körlogg starts logging.

End the trip when the phone disconnects

Create a second automation in the same way. This time choose the option for disconnecting – Disconnects for CarPlay, Is Disconnected for Bluetooth – then Run Immediately, and pick End trip under Körlogg. The trip is then in your log as soon as you switch the car off. When you switch the car off at a stop on the way, the next connection starts a new trip.

CarPlay or Bluetooth?

  • CarPlay, wired or wireless: one automation covers every car in which your iPhone uses CarPlay.
  • Bluetooth, for every other car: the car is in the Device list under the name it has in your iPhone's Bluetooth settings. You can pick one car or several.

More than one car

An automation starts the trip. With an iBeacon in each car, Körlogg Pro also puts every trip on the car you actually drove. Frequently asked questions

Do I still need “Log trips automatically”?

That is your choice. With the switch on, Körlogg also notices a drive from the phone's motion, so every trip is caught, in any car. With it off, only your automations and the Start button begin a trip – the way to keep a log of your own car and nothing else.

Does it start while Körlogg is closed?

Yes, with location access set to “Always”. iPhone starts Körlogg in the background and the trip begins.

Does the automation ask me every time?

Not with Run Immediately. With Run After Confirmation iPhone shows a notification first and waits for your tap.

What about moving the car a few metres?

A recording shorter than 300 metres is not saved as a trip, so moving the car across the yard does not fill your log.

Can I start a trip from the Home Screen?

Yes. Körlogg's widget shows this month's business distance and has a start and stop button.

How Körlogg helps

Körlogg logs your trips with the date, times, start and end address and distance; you swipe each one business or private and add the reason. With Körlogg Pro you export the log as PDF or Excel for your employer, your accountant or your tax return. Körlogg on the App Store
