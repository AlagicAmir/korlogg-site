2. What the App Does

Körlogg detects and records car trips using your phone's location and motion sensors, lets you classify trips as business or private, and produces reports (PDF and CSV) laid out after the information Skatteverket recommends for a körjournal. It also calculates mileage reimbursement (milersättning) from rates you can edit.

Whether a körjournal is accepted is decided by the authority or employer that receives it, not by us.

3. Accuracy of Recorded Trips

GPS and motion detection are not perfect. Trips can be missed, merged, split or measured a little short or long, especially in tunnels, cities with tall buildings, or when the phone's battery-saving settings limit background activity. You are responsible for reviewing and correcting the log before relying on it, and for entering odometer readings. We recommend comparing the log with your odometer regularly. We are not liable for tax, reimbursement or other consequences of an inaccurate or incomplete log.

Corrections and the change log. Kilometres and odometer readings of a trip can be corrected for 7 days after the trip, then they are fixed. For vehicles under Austrian or German rules the whole trip is fixed after those 7 days, trips still unsorted then count as private, and trips cannot be deleted, only cancelled with a reason. Every change made by hand is recorded and printed in the export, as tax authorities expect of an electronic log.

4. Your Data and Backups

Your trips are stored on your device, and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud account. Deleting the App deletes the local data; iCloud sync is a convenience, not an archive. Swedish rules require a körjournal to be kept for several years; export your reports and keep them yourself. We have no copy and cannot recover deleted data.
